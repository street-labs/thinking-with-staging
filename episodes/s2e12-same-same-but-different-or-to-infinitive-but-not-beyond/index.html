Overview

Working through the Twenty-Fourth Series ("Of the Communication of Events") of The Logic of Sense, with the full crew. The conversation stages Deleuze against Hegel — the productivity of distance versus the unity of opposites — and runs through compossibility and Leibniz, disjunction and conjunction, and Deleuze's odd grammar of infinitives ("to butterfly"). It widens out to whether first philosophy is ontology or ethics, the eternal return of the same versus difference, and Deleuze's call to "make more towns."
