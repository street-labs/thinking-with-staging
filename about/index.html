How it started

We started Thinking With… in 2020, during the depths of the pandemic. We were all stuck, largely isolated, and intellectually starving — teaching online, our usual conferences canceled, with little of the ordinary opportunity to think and talk with other people. Then, on some random day in June, John called Nathaniel and said, “hey, you should read this really smart dissertation proposal I just got from a student of mine, Nate DeProspo.” Things spiraled from there. Nathaniel and Nate met, along with John, on this new thing called “Zoom,” to talk about Nate's project — and to ask him why in the world he was so invested in G. W. F. Hegel of all people.

We kept Zooming, as a reading group. After a while we imagined our discussions were for an audience. It was something of a joke at first — us, starting a podcast on esoteric philosophy. But it got us thinking about how we'd relate to listeners. If we were to make a podcast, what would the goal be, especially since we spend most of our time teaching in a classroom? There's no shortage of material on the internet aimed at teaching. And one of the ideas we kept circling back to is that the most powerful teaching and learning we do has little to do with presenting and remembering propositions, arguments, and histories. For us, philosophy and rhetoric are about teaching styles of thinking — and we learn styles of thinking by getting swept up in a current.

We wanted our podcast to be a stream to get caught up in, for our listeners and for us. We wanted to “think with.” The question was, think with what? The answer had to be ambiguous and precise at once: think with each other, with texts, with intellectual traditions and longstanding but ever-mutating questions and concepts. We wanted to imagine that our listeners weren't taking notes for an exam but were swept up and thinking along with us — and imagining that audience shaped how we read, spoke, and positioned ourselves while recording. That's why we added the ellipsis: the whole idea was an experiment in thinking with… an ever-changing complex of things.

What's changing

After six years, and a surprising number of positive responses from listeners, we decided to change things up a little. Our goal is still the same: to provoke different styles of thinking by thinking with… But we decided to put some of our “behind the scenes” work into the foreground and to invite even more others to Think With us.

First, there's the podcast archive where you can listen and read transcripts from any of our seasons or standalone episodes. But we're also including some of the writing that circulates around the podcast – from provocations, to essays, lectures and echo responses to anything else.

The signature aspect of ThinkingWith… is the relation that occurs between us; we are always looking to invite others who are interested in such experiments, whether conceptual, visual, technological, or otherwise. If you are interested in contributing in some way, just drop us a note here.
