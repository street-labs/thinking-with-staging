Overview

Part two of Book V of The Gay Science, recorded after a long hiatus, reads Nietzsche as therapy — against the seductions of dogmatic anger and the poison of ressentiment, and toward joy, rest, and the metaphorics of dancing. The discussion turns on philosophy as the expression of a way of life (§370, §372), the will to truth becoming suspicious of itself (§346), the scholar versus the experimentalist, and "overcoming the human" — with Foucault's recent "man" in view — before landing on consciousness, the body, and the overabundance of nature.
