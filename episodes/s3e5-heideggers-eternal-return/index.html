Overview

From The Heidegger Files: a reading of Lecture X of What Is Called Thinking? on how time complicates Being, the eternal return of the same, and Nietzsche's "deliverance from revenge." The hosts ask why the "same" returns and what the "eternal" amounts to, working through Dasein and representational thinking, linear versus blurred time, and whether perspectivalism is epistemological or ontological. They close on misreadings of the eternal return and the contingency of essence and destiny.
