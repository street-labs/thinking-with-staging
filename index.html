A response to Bo Burnham's Inside

Thinking With… Bo Burnham’s Inside
Provocations October 3, 2026

As Plato pointed out in the Phaedrus, there is a difference between a love that wants to enable the loved one and a love that wants to possess the loved one. Of course, they are not at all mutually exclusive and there are all kinds of complex ways they might be combined, but the point here is simply that there is a difference between these styles of love. But it is a difference that neither the lover nor the loved one can ever be sure of (about the other or even within themselves). The difference is real, and yet is not simply knowable as something that one could point to with certainty. I think this is a difficult line to hear: differences are real and consequential without necessarily being objective or verifiable - even within oneself.
